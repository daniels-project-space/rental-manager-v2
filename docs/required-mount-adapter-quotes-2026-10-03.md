# Complete mount adapter quotes

A native model regression offered the owned Remus 100mm PL lens for £50 over two days and mentioned the required PL to L adapter without pricing it. The existing Blackmagic Full Frame booking cost £124, so the usable addition should have been £70 and the complete booking £194.

Pricing and addition quote tools now resolve the existing booking and selected listing components, find exact required adapters, look up their actual listing prices and query the complete addition basket. These quotes do not mutate a booking. Already included adapters are not charged again, and multiple lenses sharing one camera do not require separate adapters. Missing or ambiguous mounts or adapter prices prevent a complete setup quote. Native component and basket receipts reach the shared draft guard, which rejects a lens price that omits its required adapter cost.

An explicit statement that the renter owns the matching adapter avoids charging for that adapter. The statement survives unrelated follow-up messages and is removed by a direct revocation. This reads the last 60 thread messages; it is not a durable ownership register. Renter equipment remains their responsibility and never becomes owner stock.
