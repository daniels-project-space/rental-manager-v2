# Native set checks and conditional quote totals

Two fresh Lab failures had real underlying data but brittle interpretation. A Remus/adapter quote had Native £70 addition and £194 full-proposal receipts, yet “Adding both would be an additional £70, bringing your total to £194” lost its conditional scope after the first amount. A Great Joy stock call removed focal-list separators, returned unknown, and the model wrote a refusal anyway.

Price review now retains a conditional continuation across currency amounts within the same sentence. It still requires the exact Native basket, members, quantities, duration and amount. Conditional scope does not cross sentence, paragraph or semicolon boundaries and cannot certify an already-applied change.

Both stock tools now share one Native joint-check implementation. Explicit prime families/focal lists resolve from canonical inventory identities and expand within that check. The single-item stock tool also routes a recognized set directly into it using the same loaded stock snapshot. Resolution and guard review share focal-list grammar, including Oxford commas, explicit `mm` values separated by spaces, and optional anamorphic/lens descriptors. Bare unitless whitespace lists, missing members, duplicate focals and ambiguous variants remain unverified.

A verified non-rentable member is a necessary catalogue constraint: the requested set cannot be supplied regardless of addition/replacement choice. The check returns exact per-member receipts and labels that constraint without claiming every member is missing or attributing it to a calendar conflict. Positive proposals retain the existing basket and still require appropriate booking context; unresolved context stays unknown.

Validation: 1,224 passed and 14 skipped in 92 files, production build, backend typecheck and Graphify update. The actual £194 candidate passes captured-evidence replay. Native probes confirm both Great Joy spellings are non-rentable, unverified focal lists stay unknown, owned Remus sets fit alongside the retained Full Frame basket through either stock tool, and undecided positive context stays unknown. Probe orders remain unchanged and are cleaned up. Fresh deployed conversations and send dry runs are additional release evidence, not assumed from these checks.

The broader audit remains active, including legacy receipt scope, exact commercial exclusions, stale addition pricing at send time, and other stage/recommendation behavior. Real Hygglo messages, cancellations and booking changes remain gated on explicit written consent.
