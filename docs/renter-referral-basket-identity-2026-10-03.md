# Preserve equipment identity across friend basket referrals

Friend redemption already refreshed prices and checked current basket stock, but identified source listing lines by their product IDs. A product mapping changed from an FX3 to a different available camera could pass those checks and silently restore different equipment. The regression test confirms that ordinary basket stock is available after the remapping, while the new referral identity check rejects it.

Final verification failure now preserves an immutable snapshot of the tracked physical components, Native item IDs, canonical inventory identities and aggregate quantities. Cancellation still succeeds when catalogue resolution fails; an unresolved snapshot requires owner review before restoration. Redemption uses the same resolver as basket stock, compares the preserved equipment before repricing or writing the destination, then checks current stock and prices. Changed equipment, quantities or inventory identities, and legacy referrals without a snapshot, cannot be silently adopted. Standard accessories excluded from independent stock tracking remain outside this fingerprint; this is not an exhaustive kit-contents attestation. The snapshot is taken at final verification failure, not at original order creation.

Verification: 130 focused tests across verification/referrals and Lab order/atomic stock workflows, plus project/backend typechecks. New mutation tests cover an available substitute under a changed mapping, changed component quantities, changed identity labels, legacy codes and cancellation with unresolved catalogue data. Rejection leaves the destination and referral consumption unchanged. The deployed Native Lab sendTestMessage path cancelled a waiting-verification source, restored the same real catalogue TTArtisan basket at £42 to a separate inquiry, and retained idempotency without transferring verification or booking status. Both owned fixtures were cleaned. No live catalogue changes, real rental writes or paid model calls. There were no active legacy referral rows before this phase.

The broader audit remains open, including equipment identity before the failure snapshot, recommendation/specification truth, complete owner-check history and owner authentication. Real chat migration, sending and booking/cancellation adapters still require explicit written consent.
