# Lens notation and proposed price evidence audit

A live failed-verification/friend-basket audit exposed valid lens names that could not resolve: `Canon EF 16-35mm f/2.8` and `Canon EF 16–35 f2.8` failed while the native `Canon EF 16-35mm f2.8` succeeded. The shared tokenizer split a slash aperture into a different token and treated an attached millimetre unit as part of the model number. It now normalizes those notations before the existing full-coverage/unique-match gate. Mounts, aperture values and model variants remain identity tokens. Unknown or ambiguous variants still require clarification.

A separate native add-on quote contained the Canon EF 16-35mm line at £20/day, £40 for two days, and a proposed £164 basket. The route's price adapter preserved only the £164 basket and dropped the individual line. The price guard correctly withheld the £20 and £40 claims because its evidence was missing. The adapter now retains bound individual native proposal lines as well as the basket, with the native physical item/listing identity, quantity, rate, total, dates and call ID. Listing aliases require the matching native account and product ID; request arguments do not supply identity.

Proposal members, combined quantities, inclusive date duration and summed line totals must agree before any proposal evidence is emitted. Individual line rates must also reproduce their native totals before proving prices. A read-only proposal remains conditional evidence for the revised basket: it never establishes that items were added or a booking was confirmed. No guard was weakened to accept missing proof.

This phase does not claim the entire bot audit is complete. Grouped prose references, required lens adapters, optical suitability, complete multi-camera kit quantities, authentication/stage coverage, uncalibrated numeric confidence and physical phone-push durability remain audit work.
